## Summary (framing only — see Phase F of the plan for execution detail)

**Task.** Define `VfxCellContext` in `tui-vfx-types` carrying the **seven**
spatial-context fields shared across `Filter`, `Mask`, `Sampler`, and
`StyleShader` (`local_x`, `local_y`, `width`, `height`, `screen_x`,
`screen_y`, `t`). Migrate the three unbundled traits in
`tui-vfx-compositor/src/traits/` to take a single `&VfxCellContext`
parameter. Refactor `ShaderContext` (in `tui-vfx-style`) to compose
`VfxCellContext` and keep its style-only fields
(`phase: Option<Phase>`, `runtime_params`, `roles`).
**Note (v1.2.0 correction):** v1.1.0 framing said "eight fields" including
`phase`. Verified that move would have inverted dependency direction
(`AnimationPhase` lives in `tui-vfx-recipes`, downstream); plan now
omits `phase` from `VfxCellContext` and Filter/Mask/Sampler do not
receive it.

**Why.** Three sibling traits already absorbed two breaking signature
churns each (`Filter` v3.0.0, `Sampler` v2.0.0, `Mask` similar). The
next addition would re-pay the same churn for ~56 impls. Bundling
into `VfxCellContext` makes the next field addition a struct-field bump,
not a coordinated three-trait migration.

**Architectural decision (already made — sweep §6.1 Option C).**
`VfxCellContext` lives in `tui-vfx-types`. The `Vfx` prefix is mandatory
per Intention 8. `ShaderContext` composes `VfxCellContext`; runtime-params
and roles stay on `ShaderContext` only.

**Migration order (non-negotiable).** `VfxCellContext` first → refactor
`ShaderContext` → migrate `Mask` → migrate `Sampler` → migrate `Filter`
→ cross-repo audit. See plan Phase F.1–F.6.

**Companions.** Plan §3 (architectural decision), plan Phase F (execution
detail), plan §6 (Phase F risks), plan §8 (Phase F definition of done),
sweep §1.1.A and §6.1, INTENTIONS 1 / 8 / 26 / 41.
